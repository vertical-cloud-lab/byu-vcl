## Daily Atomizer Run SOP by Gage Erickson

### Gage

**Setup:**
- Turn on recording cameras.
- Put on PPE.
- Check for / or prepare material for atomizing.
- Check atomizer water, argon pressure, vacuum pump oil, and filters.
- Turn on atomizer power, compressed air, argon.
- Check atomizer inside and out for cleanliness.
- Check nozzle, guards, and sonotrode (ultrasonic system) to make sure they are properly in place.
- Reconfigure sonotrode (boosters) if necessary.
- Install US (utlrasonic) Plate.
- Test sonotrode frequency and spray IPA to test ability.
- Close chamber.
- Put in material.
- Clean seal and close furnace.

**Atomization:**
- Begin purge process.
- Atomize.
- Begin post process cooling procedure.

**Post Atomization (waiting for cooling):**
- Upload information parameters from run into https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/261
- Upload all videos from the day's run onto Youtube. Labeled "Month/Day/Year Atomizer Run Video #". Attach to Atomizer Runs playlist.
- Once at 100 degrees C turn off heat exchanger and atomizer fully. 
- Close furnace lid to protect against dust buildup.

### Ronnie

**Furnace cleaning:**
- Turn on cameras.
- Put on PPE (gloves).
- Turn on atomizer power.
- Clean out furnace.
- Check and replace nozzle / drill out nozzle if closed.
- Rebuild furnace.
- Clean seal and surface contacting seal with IPA.
- Close furnace.

**Chamber Cleaning:**
- Dawn respirator.
- open chamber with laminated paper underneath to catch powder.
- Clean chamber door, sonotrode, and US plate.
- Remove US plate if damaged or unusable (turn on compressed air to test if reusable).
- Clean out chamber, view port, crucible, and crucible stand.
- Replace US plate and tighten to 50 torr.
- Clean chamber seal and surface touching seal with IPA.
- Close chamber door air tight.

**Collection Chamber Cleaning:**
- Still wearing respirator, open collection chamber.
- Collect, bag, and mark metal splashes from splash plate.
- Clean splash plate and collection cone down to collection cup.
- Replace splash plate.
- Clean seals and surfaces touching seals with IPA.
- Close collection chamber.

**Post Atomizer Cleaning:**
- Remove collection cup.
- Deposit powder in jar and clean cup.
- Label a jar and a row in Excel sheet with 6 digit code.
- Place jar and excess aluminum in closet.
- Clean seals and surfaces contacting seals with IPA.
- Reattach cup with valve open.
- Use IPA to clean Atomizer screens and handles.
- Upload videos to Youtube labeled "Month/Day/Year Atomizer Run Video #". Attach to Atomizer Runs Playlist.

### Paul

**Lab Preparation:**
- Wipe down table and used tools with IPA.
- Organize table and place items in proper storage areas, such as powders and aluminum into the closet.
- Sweep floor of containment room except area inside yellow caution lines (Area immediately around the atomizer and in between the heat exchanger and the atomizer).
- Deposit swept material into the Solid Material Waste bucket.
- Upload parameters posted from the day's run in https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/261 to the Excel sheet.
- Check phones and Youtube for videos of the day. If uploaded, delete videos off of phones.
- Copy all Youtube video links and post them into issue #261.
- Clean phones with IPA and plug in to charge.

**Material Preparation (Only upon request):**
- Ask if any material needs to be prepared for the next day (or week).

- For aluminum crucibles: follow dimensions outlined in #248
- Pour powder into crucible inside fume hood.
- Press fit crucible lids.

- For Al 6063 rods: each day we need three 4" rods. 1 day a week cut 12 rods.

- Clean rods and crucibles with IPA and store in a sealed plastic bag in the containment closet.
