## DWH1CEygsTI — Oct 6 atomizer run, video 3 (34:42, Oct 6)

14:37–15:12 MDT. The charge is wiped with IPA and loaded beside the sealing rod, the seal wiped and the furnace closed.
One gas-wash sequence takes the oxygen to 19 ppm ("low 20s is good enough"), then Gage heats straight to 900 °C, raises
the pour pressure to 0.2 bar ("20 bar" as said), lets it settle at 840 °C for two minutes, scans the ultrasonics and
reads his checklist. The pour starts with the vibration off and the stack too high: the stream hits the upper
sonotrode, blobs and drips off. Gage pulls the stack back about three quarters of the way through and some melt
atomizes. Oxygen then reads 121 ppm. He explains what went wrong, and the cooldown to 400 °C begins. Phases: before
(charge), during (gas wash, heating, pour), troubleshooting, after (cooldown).
