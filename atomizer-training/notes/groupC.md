### Procedural steps

**Before (reconfiguring the ultrasonic stack)**
- Remove the plate, then unscrew the whole stack; order doesn't matter (00:26)
- Remove the housing from the booster — four hex screws (00:53)
- Disconnect cable and compressed air, then remove the chamber clamp (18:58)
- If the cable connector won't come out, loosen its small locking nut (19:20)
- Hold the transducer as the stack comes free; never let it drop (20:04)
- Free a seized stud with two M10 nuts jammed together (30:41)
- Flip the booster so the 1.5:1 reduction faces the chamber (33:28)
- Fit the smaller housing before reassembly — it won't pass over the transducer (33:03)
- Tighten housing screws only enough to seat the rubber seals (34:58)
- Fit the connector stud with the M8 thread and wrench flats facing up (35:32)
- Torque transducer–booster to 65, booster–sonotrode to 60, plate connector to 50; stop at the click (36:40, 40:21)
- Check the torque-wrench arrow; reverse the ratchet to tighten the other way; counter-hold with a second wrench (38:03)
- Set torque by pulling the collar and rotating; read the base scale plus the vernier (38:37)
- Connect air and cable, scan and run a test before installing in the chamber (40:26)
- Push in and lock the cable connector; unlock it before removal (40:55)
- Put the protective plate on first and check the seal before inserting the stack (43:09)
- Lock the chamber door open while installing (43:27)
- For normal use, bolt the protective cover over the transducer (43:32)
- Install the O-ring seal (44:56)
- Thread the connector fully into the sonotrode, then the plate, then the top sonotrode (46:29)
- Check flat contact and no gaps; torque the plate joint to 50 (47:47, 48:22)
- Final scan test with water on the plate (49:30)
- Connect the collection container; put the plate inside the protection plate; frames (51:42)

**After / Cleaning**
- Remove crucible, wipe it, check and clean the nozzle, reassemble (00:09)
- Vacuum the pump port and view-port crevices, then the surfaces (01:14)
- Don't grind or clean plates; dedicate a plate to one material and log it (17:51, 18:16)
- Clean the O-ring with isopropyl and paper towels (52:23)
- Store powders and graphite consumables in a desiccant dry box (10:30)

**Troubleshooting**
- Resonance/odd behaviour: check the plate/connector is centred and not bearing on the ring (16:48)
- Reverse booster lowers frequency below 40 kHz; the top sonotrode raises it; acceptable for short runs (41:40)
- Dark spot or crack at the plate edge means it is heating from tension; expect it to fail there (50:21)

## 9kn-HhXCr1o — Atomizer Training Video 9 (50 min, Sep 30)

The most complete run-through. Gage narrates the automated gas-wash purge, heat-exchanger and generator start-up, and the staged heat-up (250 °C → purge → 500 °C → purge → 850 °C), with Bartosz explaining why. After cooldown there is a full consumables tour (insulation, nozzle holders, sealing rods, nozzles, thermocouples, seals, filters, plate materials) and closing advice. ### Procedural steps

**Before / pump-down**
- Run gas wash: furnace first, then chamber; 5 cycles to −0.85 bar with argon backfill (00:05, 04:16)
- Keep overpressure in the other volume while pumping one (03:25)
- Press pressure control to bring the chamber to 150 mbar (01:41)
- Switch the heat exchanger on (red switch at back); wait for the flow alarm to clear (01:58)
- Generator start; set 250 °C (02:29)
- At 250 °C: pressure control off, vacuum pump on, gas wash again (03:02)
- Once that purge reaches −850 mbar, set 500 °C and purge again (06:13, 07:10)
- Pressure control on; raise to the 850 °C melting setpoint (12:37)

**During**
- Watch for the temperature dip and faster beeping = melting (13:30, 14:03)
- Nudge the furnace pressure a little to push the melt; if needed, control it manually with the turbo pressure (14:16, 10:43)
- When the sealing rod sinks, reduce to 800 °C (15:42)
- Wait ~2 min after full melt for overheat and mixing (15:57, 17:18)
- Start transducer cooling, rescan if needed, start vibration (19:34)
- Switch to pouring pressure and open the sealing rod (20:14)
- Pulse turbo pressure to stabilise the stream; raise amplitude by slider only if not atomizing (45:12)

**After**
- Stop vibration, sealing rod down, melting pressure, stop heating — in any order, promptly (21:39)
- Cool; below 100 °C stop cooling; vent before opening (46:11)
- Open, brush, remove the powder (28:30, 30:14)
- Inspect the nozzle for solidified metal; disassemble if a new charge wouldn't push through (30:20)
- Clean the back/furnace filters in an ultrasonic bath a few times before replacing them (36:21)
- Check the chamber–pump filter every 1–2 months (38:14)

**Troubleshooting**
- Stream too fast, un-atomized drips: smaller nozzle (0.5 mm) and/or lower amplitude (21:02, 27:47)
- Furnace pressure too low → splatter; keep a positive differential and push through a smaller hole (25:08)
- Condensation on the floor: throttle the chilled-water supply (47:07)
