# Independent review of the issue #380 report

Scope: `README.md` (short answer and sections 1–10), `FINDINGS_BRIEF.md` and `RECOMMENDATIONS.md`, checked against
the files they cite (`data/`, `companies/`, `edison/`, `gh-mining/`, `language/`, `VERIFICATION.md`, figures).
Recomputations used the repo's own CSVs and the classification rules in `scripts/robustness_checks.py` and
`scripts/openness_outcomes.py`. No existing file was changed.

Every category in the brief turned up at least one issue:

- **Numbers that don't match the cited file:** rows 7, 18, 20, 24, 28, 33, 35 and 36.
- **Internal contradictions:** rows 5, 6, 7, 16, 22, 27, 33 and 38.
- **Overstatements:** rows 1–4, 8, 9, 14–17, 19, 21, 23, 26, 30, 31, 34, 37 and 39.
- **Misleading figures:** rows 1, 4, 10, 14 and 33. No broken links: every local link and image path in the three
  documents resolves.
- **Missing caveats:** rows 3, 4, 10–13, 15, 25, 29 and 32.
