## We believe low-cost, high-quality, and easy-to-use tools can expand access to science.

Tool Foundry is a Luminary Labs initiative funded by grants from the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation and Schmidt Futures to expand access to science.

The Tool Foundry [accelerator](https://www.toolfoundry.org/accelerator/) is a four-month program designed to help a diverse cohort of teams iterate and scale accessible and powerful scientific discovery tools.

### Accelerator at a glance

Accelerator cohort:

[5 teams](https://www.toolfoundry.org/accelerator/#meet-the-cohort)

Funding amount:

$50,000 per team

Accelerator duration:

4 months

Showcase:

November 18, 2019

[More details](https://www.toolfoundry.org/accelerator/)

## Meet the cohort

Based on the [selection criteria](https://www.toolfoundry.org/accelerator/#selection-criteria) — including team ability, growth potential, and vision — Tool Foundry invited five teams working on accessible tools in application areas including biomedical imaging, health and environmental monitoring, and digital fabrication to join the accelerator cohort.

## How the Tool Foundry accelerator works

The four-month accelerator will provide participating teams with resources and networks to develop, iterate, and strengthen their tools, while building a foundation for sustainable growth.

[Learn more](https://www.toolfoundry.org/accelerator/)

### IMPORTANT DATES

May 30, 2019

#### Applications due

Applications closed at 5pm EDT on May 30, 2019.

July 9, 2019

#### Cohort candidate presentations

Candidates shared their tools with our expert selection committee.

July 29, 2019

#### Accelerator kickoff

Let’s get started! Virtual introductions to fellow cohort members and accelerator resources.

#### Boot Camp

Two days of in-person cohort collaboration and learning at Autodesk Technology Center in San Francisco, in partnership with Autodesk Foundation.

A celebratory event in New York City. Cohort teams will present their tools to press and partners.
