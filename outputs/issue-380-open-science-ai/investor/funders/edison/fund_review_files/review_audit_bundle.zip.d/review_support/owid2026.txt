Our World in Data is a collaborative effort between [researchers at the University of Oxford](https://www.oxfordmartin.ox.ac.uk/global-development), who are the scientific editors of the website content; and the non-profit organization [Global Change Data Lab](https://global-change-data-lab.org/) (GCDL), which publishes and maintains the website and the data tools that make our work possible.

GCDL is a registered charity in England and Wales (Charity Number 1186433). Our work is funded through grants and donations from readers who support our project.

### Grants

We are supported by grants from foundations including the [Wellcome Trust](https://wellcome.org), the [Gates Foundation](https://www.gatesfoundation.org), the [Skip Foundation](https://www.skipfoundation.org), [Coefficient Giving](https://coefficientgiving.org), and the [Mohn Westlake Foundation](https://www.themohnwestlakefoundation.co.uk).

In the past, we have also received grants from the [Quadrature Climate Foundation](https://www.qc.foundation), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com), [GiveWell](https://www.givewell.org), among others.

You can find a full breakdown of our funding, as well as our audited financial statements, in our [Trustees’ Annual Report](https://assets.ourworldindata.org/uploads/2026/06/2025%20Annual%20report.pdf). In this document, you can also find details about our organizational structure and the most recent report on how we are spending our funds.[1](https://ourworldindata.org/funding#note-1)

### Donations

Our work is also made possible by supporters who donate directly on our website. More than 4,000 people have donated over the past couple of years.

When donating, we ask each supporter whether they agree to be listed publicly, and most have agreed – here are the names and dedications provided by these readers. We update this donor list quarterly.

Huge thanks to everyone!
