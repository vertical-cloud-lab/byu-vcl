# Outside-funding audit notes

Research cutoff: **2026-11-26**. I researched **40 ventures** and wrote **204 CSV rows**, including **2 none_found rows**. **20 venture histories remain INCOMPLETE** because material early rounds, cash receipt, investor allocation or overlapping disclosures could not be settled; this does not mean those ventures were skipped. The other 20 received reasonable research coverage, not a guarantee that undisclosed funding does not exist.

I use only the requested ten CSV columns and the deal_rules.md event types. I preserve input keys/names exactly. Low confidence means a disclosed but weakly corroborated record, not confirmed receipt. Unallocated-backer rows preserve named funding relationships without inventing a round. Do not sum this file as verified lifetime capital: it includes possible overlapping closings, credit commitments, secondary sales, product/predecessor campaigns and ongoing-support programmes. I exclude unverified solicitations/reservations, ordinary sales, noncash incubator membership, acquisitions, founder capital and a cancelled unpaid award.

Dates are public announcement/disclosure months when established; blanks mean the month cannot be supported. Explicitly labelled campaign/commitment dates are distinguished below. Grants with only project-start dates retain those starts in labels, not fabricated announcement dates. Amounts are nominal whole USD, rounded half-up where necessary; blanks are not zero. For 31 non-USD amounts I use a historical Frankfurter v1 rate on the 15th of the disclosed month (or the returned preceding business day); project-start month is used only for conversion where award-announcement timing is unavailable. Original amounts, exact rate dates and endpoints are recorded below. This is a transparent near-date approximation, not an exact transaction-date conversion. Partial investor tickets are not substituted for an unknown whole-round total. USD headline lower/upper thresholds and campaign off-platform commitments remain qualified in labels/notes. Named sponsors are not necessarily equity holders; IPO underwriters are not treated as investors.
