### MakerBot Industries, LLC

founded 2009 · US · origin: founder_startup · openness 3→0 · acquired · $403.0M (acquisition_price, 2013-06) · funding $13.0M · confidence: medium · tool: WebSearch

*Computed:* age 17 yrs · 1 closing / 0 opening moves · latest value ÷ funding raised ≈ 31.0×

**What openness did.** Open RepRap-derived designs and Thingiverse gave MakerBot a large community and rapid sales (22k printers by 2013), turning about $13M of venture money into a $403M stock exit four years after founding. Closing in 2012 alienated RepRap developers, and it did not protect the business: after the acquisition the closed 5th-generation line and Smart Extruder failures led to layoffs, a lawsuit and Stratasys writing off all MakerBot goodwill by the end of 2015.

**Takeaway.** MakerBot is the canonical 'open, then closed, then absorbed' arc: openness and community created the brand and the exit value, and closing (just before the Stratasys deal) traded community goodwill for proprietary control that did not prevent rapid value destruction. Within seven years of the $403M deal it was folded into Ultimaker as a minority-owned brand.

**Timeline.** 2012: open-source RepRap-derived designs (Cupcake, Thing-O-Matic) → closed hardware and software (Replicator 2, MakerWare) (closing); 2013: acquisition $403.0M, Stratasys Ltd.; 2022: merger, Ultimaker B.V. (NPM Capital)

**Risk events.** 2012: Replicator 2 closed; RepRap community backlash and 'Occupy Thingiverse'; 2012: TangiBot Kickstarter clone of the open Replicator cited as a trigger for closing; 2015: Smart Extruder clogging problems; two rounds of layoffs and declining revenue (Fortune); 2015-07: securities class action against Stratasys over MakerBot disclosures (dismissed with prejudice by July 2016); 2015: Stratasys goodwill impairment of $150.4M on MakerBot in Q1; no MakerBot goodwill left by 2015-12-31; 2016-04: MakerBot laid off its manufacturing workforce and outsourced printer production to China

**Robustness.** No longer independent: a brand inside privately held UltiMaker (NPM Capital majority, Stratasys minority). Closed products depend on UltiMaker for software and cloud support (published end-of-support dates). Thingiverse, the community asset it built while open, is the most durable legacy.

**Physical-science relevance.** Early open MakerBots and Thingiverse helped spread desktop 3D printing into university labs and makerspaces; the current brand targets K-12 education (MakerBot Nebula learning platform).
