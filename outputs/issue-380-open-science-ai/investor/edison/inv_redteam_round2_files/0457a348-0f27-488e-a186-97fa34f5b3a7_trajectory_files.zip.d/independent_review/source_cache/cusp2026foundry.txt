# Launching our ‘AI Materials Foundry’ — and $450 million Series B

![Launching our ‘AI Materials Foundry’ — and $450 million Series B](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/ho74vmlz/production/8c9dd3da26c30da6d4b83a57dbf5db2f5b76a715-2168x1080.png?w=2322&h=1156&q=85&auto=format "width=2168 | height=1080")

* [X](https://x.com/intent/post?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcusp.ai%2Fblog%2Flaunching-our-ai-materials-foundry-and-450-million-series-b&text=Launching%20our%20%E2%80%98AI%20Materials%20Foundry%E2%80%99%E2%80%8A%E2%80%94%E2%80%8Aand%20%24450%20million%20Series%20B)
*

Today marks a milestone for the acceleration of materials discovery as we launch the ‘AI Materials Foundry’, a global network of data, labs, compute and scientific expertise for the design of new materials, alongside announcing our $450 million fundraise.

We’re delighted to be joined by more than 45 leaders in their fields as founding partners of the AI Materials Foundry, including **NVIDIA, Meta, Samsung, Hyundai Motor Group, Henkel, Applied Materials, Tokyo Electron** and **Lam Research** among others, across the United States, Asia-Pacific and Europe.

If we don’t make progress fast, the next 50 years of industrial progress will be constrained by a single challenge: the world needs materials that don’t yet exist. That’s what we’re on a mission to solve — combining frontier agentic AI with deep domain expertise, exclusive data access and close customer partnerships.

You can read more about this initiative here on [our website](https://www.cusp.ai/)and in our [announcement.](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260716196996/en/CuspAI-Launches-AI-Materials-Foundry-a-Global-Network-to-Accelerate-Breakthrough-Discoveries) Thanks to Bloomberg for covering the story [here.](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/bezos-backs-startup-focused-on-material-discovery-for-chipmaking)

It comes as we also announce our latest fundraise, a $450 million Series B led **Kleiner Perkins** and **NEA**, with significant participation from **Jeff Bezos’ Bezos Expeditions**. The round values Cusp at **$2.6 billion** and will allow us to move faster still across the US, APAC and Europe.

Additional new investors include Glade Brook Capital Partners, Lux Capital, AMD Ventures, Tru Arrow Partners, StepStone, Britain’s Sovereign AI Venture Fund, the Netherlands’ Invest-NL and John Doerr.

Existing investors Temasek, Basis Set Ventures, Giant Ventures, Touring Capital, Prosus, Phoenix Court and Northzone also participated. The round follows our $100M+ Series A less than a year ago, led by NEA and Temasek.

> “Most big leaps in technology come down to a material, and the next set, from cheaper carbon capture to semiconductors and cleaner water, is stuck waiting on materials nobody has discovered yet. CuspAI built a search engine that changes that. What sets Max, Chad, and the team apart is that they design for materials that can actually be built, not just ones a model can dream up. I believe CuspAI’s technology can define the AI-driven materials revolution, and Kleiner Perkins is thrilled to co-lead their Series B.”

*Josh Coyne, Partner at Kleiner Perkins*

> “Accelerating materials discovery is one of the most critical challenges of our time, with profound implications across semiconductors, energy and advanced manufacturing. What sets CuspAI apart is their ability to bridge the gap between frontier AI and the physical world, winning them industry leaders as their early partners. Since NEA co-led the Series A, we’ve seen Chad and Max’s relentless focus and ability to build a world-class team. We’re proud to double down on our investment as CuspAI transforms how the world’s most vital industries design and deploy next-generation materials.”

*Philip Chopin, Managing Director, Head of Europe, NEA*

> “Materials discovery has quietly been a bottleneck behind progress in energy, semiconductors, and climate. What’s remarkable about Cusp is that a single AI platform is already solving hard problems across industries-from stripping forever chemicals from drinking water to designing next-generation chip materials. The shift to AI-led discovery is real, and Chad Edwards, Max Welling, and the team are at the forefront of unleashing unprecedented technological innovation.”

*John Doerr, Doerr Capital*

In parallel, we also continue to grow our incredible team, with a new office in Singapore and people in Cambridge, England (where Chad is based), Amsterdam (where Max is based), Berlin, Tokyo and the United States.

We’re delighted to have the additional expertise of **John Giannandrea** (former Apple and Google exec), who’s helping set up US foundry operations, and our latest advisory board member, **Abhi Talwalkar,** AMD Board Member & Chairman of Lam Research.

The opportunity in front of us has never been more urgent, or more exciting. New materials can unlock performance, sustainability and new solutions to existing intractable problems — the world simply can’t make the progress it needs to with known materials, that much is clear.

We appreciate the support of everyone who’s on this journey with us.

*— Dr Chad Edwards & Prof Max Welling, Co-founders, CuspAI*
