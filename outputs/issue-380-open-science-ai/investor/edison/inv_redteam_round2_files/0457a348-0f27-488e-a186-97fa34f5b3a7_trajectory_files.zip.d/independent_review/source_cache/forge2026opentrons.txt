## Company details

Opentrons creates robots designed to automate processes previously handled by hand by biologists. Opentron's liquid-handling robots can handle pipetting, which is typically done by hand. Opentrons was founded in 2013 by Chiu Chau, Will Canine, and Nicholas Wagner and is headquartered in New York, NY.
