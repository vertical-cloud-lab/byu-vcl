# q1_sota_electrolyte_sdl

## Question

State of the art: which self-driving laboratories / autonomous experimentation platforms have been demonstrated for LIQUID ELECTROLYTE FORMULATION, where an automated liquid-dosing system mixes multi-component solutions and IONIC CONDUCTIVITY is measured in a closed loop to select the next composition? Name the specific platforms (e.g. Clio/Dragonfly, Otto Chemistry, AMANDA/Autonomous Energy Materials Discovery, ChemOS, Chemspeed-based electrolyte platforms, A-Lab, Argonne/NREL electrolyte robots) with DOIs and publication years. For each: what was dosed (liquid only, or powders/salts too), how conductivity was measured (dip probe, flow cell, four-electrode, impedance/potentiostat), how many compositions per campaign, what optimizer was used, and what the reported speedup or sample-count reduction versus grid/one-factor-at-a-time was. Distinguish clearly between aqueous and non-aqueous (glovebox) systems.

## Answer

## Self-Driving Laboratories for Liquid Electrolyte Formulation with Ionic Conductivity Feedback: State of the Art

### Overview

A systematic search of the literature reveals that only a small number of platforms have demonstrated the full closed-loop cycle of automated liquid-dosing → multi-component electrolyte mixing → ionic conductivity measurement → optimizer-guided next-composition selection. Several additional platforms automate electrolyte formulation and electrochemical characterization but optimize a different target (solubility, cell cycling, or product selectivity) rather than conductivity. The following sections describe each platform, distinguishing those with genuine closed-loop conductivity optimization from those with related but distinct feedback loops.

### 1. Clio / Dragonfly — Non-Aqueous, Glovebox (Dave et al., 2022)

**The closest match to the user's query.** Clio is a custom-built robotic platform operating inside a dry-argon glovebox (H₂O < 10 ppm) that autonomously formulates non-aqueous Li-ion battery electrolytes and measures ionic conductivity in a closed loop (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 1-7, dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 16-21).

- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41467-022-32938-1 (Nature Communications, 2022)
- **What was dosed:** Liquids only — premade feeder solutions of LiPF₆ dissolved in ethylene carbonate (EC), dimethyl carbonate (DMC), ethyl methyl carbonate (EMC), and diethyl carbonate (DEC); up to 19 feeder solutions; salt molality (0–2 mol/kg) and ternary solvent mass fractions were varied. No dry-powder dosing during campaigns (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 7-12, dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 21-31).
- **Conductivity measurement:** Custom PTFE flow-through cell with symmetric platinum electrodes connected to a PalmSens4 potentiostat/impedance analyzer; impedance spectroscopy at five frequencies (14–800 kHz); resistance determined at the frequency with the smallest phase angle; cell constant calibrated with acetonitrile standard; triplicate measurements with ±1.3% mean absolute percentage error (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 16-21).
- **Compositions per campaign:** 42 total experiments over two work-days; conductivity optimum (~13.7 mS/cm for EC:DMC 40:60 with 0.9 m LiPF₆ at 26–28 °C) was identified within ~15 experiments; a separate repeatability study covered 120 samples (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 1-7, dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 7-12, dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 12-16).
- **Optimizer:** Dragonfly, an open-source Bayesian optimization package using a Gaussian-process surrogate with Thompson sampling, expected improvement, top-two EI, and upper-confidence-bound acquisition functions, plus interspersed random exploration (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 7-12).
- **Speedup:** ~6× time acceleration compared with random search on the same robot; ~3× workflow efficiency versus an 8 h/day human researcher; 42 experiments versus a nominally 1,000-candidate exhaustive grid represents ~96% fewer samples (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 1-7, dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 12-16).

### 2. CMU Autonomous Electrochemical Test Stand — Aqueous (Whitacre et al., 2019)

This is the aqueous-electrolyte precursor to Clio, developed at Carnegie Mellon University and designed for machine-learning-informed electrolyte optimization (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-1, whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-3).

- **DOI:** 10.1149/2.0521916jes (J. Electrochem. Soc., 2019); aqueous benchmarking: 10.1149/2.0142001jes (Dave et al., 2020)
- **What was dosed:** Liquids only — up to 10 solvated-salt stocks or pure solvents; aqueous LiNO₃, NaNO₃, Li₂SO₄, Na₂SO₄, and DI water; 7 mL binary blends in 1 mL increments (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 3-5, whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-3).
- **Conductivity measurement:** Flow-through Topac K = 1.0 four-pole glass/Pt conductivity probe in custom PTFE housing, read by a Consort C3410 dual-channel analyzer; temperature-compensated to 20 °C; standard error ~0.5 mS/cm; separate three-electrode Pt/Pt/Ag-AgCl cell with PalmSens potentiostat for voltage-stability windows (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-3, dave2020benchmarkingconductivitypredictions pages 4-9).
- **Compositions per campaign:** Campaign table lists 75, 800, 70, 70, and 70 tests (totaling ~1,085); each test ~7 min; two-dimensional searches completed in <1 day (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 3-5, whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 5-7).
- **Optimizer:** HTTP/Python API designed for integration with Dragonfly Bayesian optimization; the 2019 paper primarily demonstrated autonomous data gathering, with full ML integration described as future work (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-1, hu2025artificialintelligence‐drivendevelopment pages 14-14, whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 3-3).
- **Speedup:** Searches that would take much longer manually were completed in <1 day; no defensible numerical speedup versus grid or OFAT was explicitly reported (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 5-7).

### 3. Helmholtz Institute Münster / Stein Robotic HTE System — Non-Aqueous, N₂ Atmosphere (Rahmanian et al., 2023)

A high-throughput experimentation system for non-aqueous battery electrolyte formulation with automated conductivity measurement via EIS (rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 1-2, rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 2-3).

- **DOI:** 10.1038/s41597-023-01936-3 (Scientific Data, 2023); related one-shot active-learning study: 10.1002/batt.202200228 (Rahmanian et al., 2022); related ML-aided optimization: 10.1039/d3ta06249j (Yan et al., 2024)
- **What was dosed:** Both liquids and solids — gravimetric dosing of EC, PC, EMC (liquids), and LiPF₆ (solid salt) into aluminium or polymer vials with automated closure, mixing, heating, and electrode immersion (rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 1-2, rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 2-3).
- **Conductivity measurement:** Automated electrochemical impedance spectroscopy (EIS) after thermal equilibration from −30 to 60 °C in 10 °C steps; 40 mV AC excitation over 20 kHz–50 Hz; MADAP software performed Arrhenius analysis (rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 2-3).
- **Compositions per campaign:** Up to 96 formulations in 8 hours; dataset comprises 504 formulation JSON files and 5,035 individual experiments/measurements; the Flores et al. symbolic-regression study used 3,626 individual measurements aggregated into 859 data points (rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 1-2, rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 2-3, flores2022learningthelaws pages 2-3).
- **Optimizer:** The 2023 dataset paper did not incorporate a closed-loop optimizer (MADAP is downstream analysis software). The related 2022 publication used one-shot active learning for globally optimal conductivity. The system operated in a controlled N₂ atmosphere (rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 2-3).
- **Speedup:** 96 formulations per 8 hours represents high throughput; no explicit grid/OFAT speedup was reported in the available evidence.

### 4. Self-Driving CO₂ Electrolyser — Aqueous (Deng et al., 2026)

This platform demonstrates closed-loop electrolyte optimization but with ethylene selectivity (not conductivity) as the feedback signal (deng2026selfdrivingco2electrolysera pages 19-26, deng2026selfdrivingco2electrolyser pages 19-26, deng2026selfdrivingco2electrolyserd pages 1-5).

- **DOI:** Not yet established in the available record (2026 manuscript).
- **What was dosed:** Liquids only — aqueous Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, and Cs⁺ stocks dispensed by precision syringe pumps through a passive four-way mixing junction; DI-water flushing between formulations; no powder dosing.
- **Measurement:** Inline DGS2 C₂H₄ gas sensor (not ionic conductivity); GC validation.
- **Compositions per campaign:** 45 unique compositions per current-density condition, initialized from 22 training points; three current densities studied; quaternary design space >170,000 compositions at 1% resolution (deng2026selfdrivingco2electrolysera pages 19-26, deng2026selfdrivingco2electrolyser pages 19-26).
- **Optimizer:** TuRBO (trust-region Bayesian optimization) with Gaussian-process surrogate (deng2026selfdrivingco2electrolyser pages 19-26).
- **Speedup:** Each formulation evaluated in <10 min; blended compositions improved relative C₂H₄ selectivity by 5–10% over best single-cation systems; no explicit grid/OFAT sample reduction reported.

### 5. Additional Related Platforms (Not Strict Conductivity-Closed-Loop)

**ODACell (Yik et al., 2023; DOI: 10.1039/D3DD00058C):** An affordable, modular robotic system for automated electrolyte formulation, coin-cell assembly, and galvanostatic cycling using Opentrons OT-2 liquid handling and Dobot robotic arms. Demonstrated with aqueous LiClO₄/DMSO model electrolytes. Measures cycling performance, not standalone ionic conductivity. No closed-loop optimizer; 83 cells assembled in six batches with ~2% capacity RSD (yik2023automatedelectrolyteformulation pages 2-4, yik2023automatedelectrolyteformulation pages 1-2, yik2023automatedelectrolyteformulation pages 4-6).

**Electrolab (Oh et al., 2023; DOI: 10.1016/j.device.2023.100103):** An open-source, modular platform for automated characterization of redox-active electrolytes. Uses an XYZ gantry robot, multiplexed microelectrode arrays, and Python/Arduino control. Demonstrated voltammetric screening (200 voltammograms in 2 hours) and solution titration, not bulk ionic conductivity. Bayesian optimization proposed for future work; glovebox-compatible version planned (oh2023theelectrolaban pages 1-3, oh2023theelectrolaban pages 9-10).

**SALSA (Zhang et al., 2026; DOI: 10.1039/D5DD00516G):** A ~US$100 modular add-on to the Clio/ElyteOS platform for automated salt solubility assessment. Uses a 3D-printed Archimedes-screw solid doser and computer-vision dissolution detection. Demonstrated with NaFSI in ACN, DME, and EMC. Measures solubility, not conductivity (zhang2026salsaalowcost pages 1-2, zhang2026salsaalowcost pages 2-3).

**Auto-MISCHBARES (Rahmanian et al., 2024; DOI: 10.26434/chemrxiv-2024-rb9j7):** An autonomous millimetre-scale battery research system using a Hamilton Microlab 600 syringe and HELAO active-learning software. Full details were not obtainable, and ionic-conductivity measurement was not established in the available evidence (soni2025accelerateddevelopmentof pages 15-18).

**PNNL Robotic Solubility Platform (Noh et al., 2024; DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-47070-5):** A closed-loop platform for discovering electrolyte solvents for non-aqueous redox-flow batteries. Dosed both solid redox-active molecules and organic solvents; measured solubility by quantitative NMR, not conductivity. Used Bayesian optimization with GP regression; screened <10% of >2,000 candidates, achieving >90% sample reduction and >13× throughput advantage (noh2024anintegratedhighthroughput pages 2-3, noh2024anintegratedhighthroughput pages 1-2).

### Key Observations

1. **True closed-loop conductivity optimization** has been demonstrated by very few platforms. The clearest example is **Clio/Dragonfly** (Dave et al., 2022), which is the only platform in the retrieved literature that combines automated liquid dosing, in-line ionic conductivity measurement, and Bayesian-optimization-driven next-experiment selection in a single closed loop for battery electrolyte formulation (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 1-7).

2. **Aqueous vs. non-aqueous distinction:** The CMU Autonomous Test Stand (Whitacre et al., 2019) operated with aqueous electrolytes at ambient conditions (whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-1, whitacre2019anautonomouselectrochemical pages 1-3). Clio (Dave et al., 2022) operated with non-aqueous carbonate electrolytes inside a dry-argon glovebox (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 16-21). The Helmholtz HTE system operated under N₂ atmosphere with non-aqueous carbonates (rahmanian2023conductivityexperimentsfor pages 2-3).

3. **Platforms named in the query but not matching the specific scope:** A-Lab is a solid-state inorganic synthesis robot (not liquid electrolyte formulation); ChemOS is orchestration software used by MEDUSA for metal-ligand complexes (not electrolyte conductivity); Otto Chemistry is an organic synthesis robot; none of these were found to have demonstrated liquid electrolyte formulation with conductivity-closed-loop optimization in the retrieved literature (soni2025accelerateddevelopmentof pages 15-18).

4. **Chemspeed-based electrolyte platforms:** Although the Helmholtz/Münster group's system handles both liquid and solid dosing under inert atmosphere—capabilities consistent with Chemspeed hardware—the retrieved papers do not explicitly identify the robot manufacturer. The associated Krishnamoorthy et al. (2022; DOI: 10.1002/cmtd.202200008) data-driven analysis of high-throughput conductivity experiments was referenced but the full paper was not obtainable.

5. **Sample-count reductions:** The most quantitative claim is from Clio/Dragonfly: ~96% fewer samples versus exhaustive grid search and ~6× time acceleration versus random search (dave2022autonomousoptimizationof pages 1-7). The PNNL solubility platform (Noh et al., 2024) reported >90% sample reduction for solubility screening (noh2024anintegratedhighthroughput pages 1-2).
