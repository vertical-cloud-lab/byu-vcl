W = pill width (x) at that height, L = length (y), band = how far down from the top the pill is
at its full width (below that, the 45-degree flare steps it back to v2_1's 9.8 mm at z = -6).
'below z=-6 vs v2_1' is the largest area by which any section from z = -6.2 to -14.5 differs from
v2_1's: zero means the transition, latch arms, barbs and ends are v2_1's.

Latch in a 10.5 mm slot (v2_1's, so the same for all eight keys and for the keys printed on 2026-11-19 bar the
chamfer fix):
  barbs reach 0.50 mm past the slot edge (they reach 0.75 in the 10.0 mm slot they were drawn for)
  to clip in, each arm moves 0.50 mm: 3.7 N, 0.90 % peak strain (FEA, linear: 5.5 N and 1.35 % at 0.75 mm)
  the ramps meet the slot's bottom edge at z = -9.46: no vertical play on a deck 9.46 mm or thicker
     9.0 mm deck: 0.46 mm of vertical play
     9.5 mm deck: arms held 0.03 mm in, key pulled down 0.2 N
    10.0 mm deck: arms held 0.31 mm in, key pulled down 2.7 N

Lateral play of the rack at each key = the clearance in width, so 0.5 -> 0.3 -> 0.1 -> 0 mm. The keys are
225 mm apart, so the rack can also turn by up to clearance / 225 rad: 0.13, 0.08, 0.03 and 0 degrees.
