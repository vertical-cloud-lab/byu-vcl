6. Availability, asking rate, term, and the tenant-improvement allowance.

We need roughly 20,000-40,000 ft². The tall items are vertical lift modules: 13 ft units now, and possibly 50 ft
units later.

## 3. To Lehi City (planning, building and the fire marshal), before choosing a site

**Subject:** Pre-application question: a research lab with metal powders in an industrial zone

Hello,

We're at an early stage of looking for a site for a research and light-manufacturing lab and would like to
understand Lehi's requirements before narrowing down buildings. The lab would:

- use small amounts of aluminium alloy powder (atomized and 3D printed), which falls under NFPA 484;
- store argon and nitrogen, probably in bulk tanks outside;
- run laser powder-bed printers, a wire EDM, furnaces and gloveboxes;
- house automated storage units up to about 50 ft tall.

Could you tell us:

1. which zones allow this use, and whether it would need a conditional use permit;
2. which IBC and IFC editions and local amendments apply, and how you usually handle a hazardous-materials room
   (control areas, or an H occupancy) in an existing warehouse;
3. whether Lehi offers a pre-submittal or development review meeting, and how to request one;
4. height limits in those zones, for a high-bay section of about 55-60 ft.
