## Xact Metal (State College, PA)

- **Founded 2017**; launch announced May/June 2017 at Penn State's Innovation Park, where it is still headquartered. Founders: Matt Woods (CTO; built his first printer in a dorm room for ~$700, interned at SpaceX and Penn State CIMP-3D), Juan Mario Gomez (CEO, ex-GE — grew a GE business to ~$250M sales; still CEO as of Oct 2025), and possibly a third co-founder (Jonathon Hollahan, single-source, UNVERIFIED). Penn State CIMP-3D co-director Timothy Simpson is an adviser. [PSU News](https://www.psu.edu/news/academics/story/xact-metals-3d-printing-technology-launches), [3DPI 2025](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/xact-metal-reports-double-digit-growth-and-expands-sales-team-in-2025-245262/)
- **Funding**: remarkably lightly funded — ~$185K press-documented from Ben Franklin Technology Partners (2017) plus an undisclosed Garber Venture Capital Fund amount and a pandemic PPP loan; CB Insights totals "$250K over 4 rounds." No Series A or institutional VC round found anywhere. **Valuation: not public** (PitchBook paywalled; no press figure). Effectively bootstrapped relative to peers. [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/xact-metal-inc), [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/xact-metal/financials)
- **Size**: ~15 employees (third-party estimate, UNVERIFIED exact count). Revenue not public; known datapoints: a brutal ~$10K-order quarter during COVID (no layoffs taken), Q4 2024 second-highest order revenue ever, Q1 2025 record (+60% YoY), Q2/Q3 2025 each +30% YoY. [RocketReach](https://rocketreach.co/xact-metal-profile_b454fccffc93da85), [3DPI interview](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/from-aviation-to-3d-printing-xact-metals-journey-through-pandemic-and-beyond-232718/), [Metal AM](https://www.metal-am.com/xact-metal-achieves-record-first-quarter-revenue-up-60/)
- **Milestones**: first shipment Dec 2018; XM200C ($80K) / XM200S ($130K) in 2018; pivot to galvo architecture with XM200G in 2021 (from ~$90K); XM300G announced IMTS 2022 (from $200K); 100th printer order May 2023; 150th unit shipped Dec 2024; XM200G µHD micro-LPBF launched Formnext Nov 2025 (shipping mid-2026). 26+ resellers across ~48 countries; partnerships with Materialise (since founding), Oqton, Dynamism, System Create (Japan), Uddeholm. [TCT 100th](https://www.tctmagazine.com/additive-manufacturing-3d-printing-news/metal-additive-manufacturing-news/xact-metal-celebrates-100th%C2%A0metal-3d-printer-order/), [150th](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/xact-metal-reports-strong-order-revenue-and-its-150th-system-installed-235951/), [µHD launch](https://www.tctmagazine.com/xact-metal-launches-xm200g-mhd-metal-3d-printer-xm300g-development-continues/), [trade.gov](https://www.trade.gov/success-story/xact-metal-leverages-us-commercial-service-support-propel-international-growth)
- **Health**: no layoffs, distress, or closure reports found 2017–2026; sales team expanded 2025; survived COVID without downsizing.

## Mastrex (Mount Laurel, NJ)

- **The brand has existed only since January 5, 2026**, formed by merging two low-profile NJ machine sellers, Vulcan USA LLC and Burgmaster; public debut at CES 2026. CEO/co-founder Ilay Fridland. Moving most operations to San Diego while keeping NJ offices (as of Apr 2026). [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/05/3213256/0/en/Vulcan-and-Burgmaster-Merge-to-Form-Mastrex-Strengthening-the-Future-of-American-Manufacturing.html), [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/325627/rapid-democratizing-metal-lpbf-mastrex/)
- Predecessor business "began selling machines over a decade ago" (polymer printers, CNC mills, fiber laser cutters) and started developing its own LPBF tech only ~2023. Products: MX100 $39K, MX120 $49K, MX150/MX220 dual-laser, MX300 $185K (mid-2026), MX400 $360K, MX800 in development; claims US design/assembly; 2-yr warranty. [3DPI MX300](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/mastrex-launches-mx300-calls-it-industrys-most-affordable-lpbf-metal-3d-printer-252022/)
- **No ownership, funding, valuation, or headcount data is public anywhere** (UNVERIFIED). The relationship to the historic Burgmaster brand (1946 turret-drill maker, defunct 1980s) is unverified. No foreign parent/OEM connection could be substantiated in either direction.

## Aconity3D (Herzogenrath, Germany)

- Founded **2014** as a Fraunhofer ILT (Aachen) spinout — first machine built in a container at the institute; led by Dr. Yves Hagedorn. Independent and founder-owned; managing directors Hagedorn, Görres, Blom; corporate structure includes Aconity Holding (2019). No public VC or valuation. [Laser Region Aachen](https://laserregionaachen.de/beteiligte-partner/partner/unternehmenssteckbriefe/aconity), [3DPI Q&A](https://3dprintingindustry.com/news/qa-yves-hagedorn-managing-director-at-aconity3d-137744/)
- **51–200 employees** (LinkedIn band; 31 on German filings end-2021, >50 reported in 2018). US subsidiary AconityUS established 2018 at University of Texas at El Paso. [LaserFocusWorld](https://www.laserfocusworld.com/industrial-laser-solutions/article/14220744/additive-manufacturing-company-aconity3d-establishes-us-operations), [UTEP](https://www.utep.edu/aconity/)
- Health signals positive: moved into own building Oct 2023; AconityWEEK 2026 user conference drew 82+ participants from 20+ countries; AconityCONTROL closed-loop process control demoed 2026; no distress signals found. [Aconity news](https://news.aconity3d.com/)

## 2oneLab (Darmstadt, Germany)

- Founded **June 2021** by Markus Wolf (physicist, 10+ yrs additive laser systems) and Ran Reznik; private GmbH, founder-owned, share capital €11,111; no public funding or valuation. US arm 2onelab Inc. (Newnan, GA). [2oneLab about](https://2onelab.com/about/), [Handelsregister](https://www.online-handelsregister.de/handelsregisterauszug/he/Darmstadt/HRB/102296/2oneLab-GmbH)
- **Micro-company: ~2–10 employees** (directory estimates). 2CREATE launched Feb 2022 (250 W, Ø110×100 mm); 2CREATE Plus (400 W) later; strong dental focus; distributed via Proto3000 (N. America). Active at trade shows, no distress signals — but minimal organizational depth behind support commitments. [3druck](https://3druck.com/en/printers-and-products/2onelab-2create-26106265/), [Proto3000](https://proto3000.com/product/2onelab-2create/)

## One Click Metal (Tamm, Germany) — comparable compact-LPBF peer

- Founded **April 2019** as a TRUMPF spinout; **INDEX Group** (2,000+ employee CNC maker, est. 1914) took a majority stake in 2021 with TRUMPF retaining a minority — i.e., real corporate backing at a similar machine price point (MPRINT bundle ~€100K; PROline ~$180K at Formnext 2025). ~20 employees. US channel expanding via TriMech (Oct 2024) and MasterGraphics. No insolvency or layoff reports found through mid-2026. [Metal AM (INDEX)](https://www.metal-am.com/index-acquires-majority-interest-in-one-click-metal/), [PRNewswire (TriMech)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/trimechs-new-partnership-with-one-click-metal-will-bring-user-friendly-metal-3d-printing-to-more-north-american-businesses-302286171.html), [All3DP (PROline)](https://all3dp.com/4/at-180k-one-click-metals-new-powder-bed-fusion-eyes-industrial-3d-printing/)

## EOS (Krailling, Germany)

- Founded **April 1989** by Dr. Hans J. Langer (with Dr. Hans Steinbichler); the industry's longest-running independent AM company, still **private and family-owned** (Langer family); daughter Marie Langer CEO since Oct 2019. [EOS history](https://www.eos.info/about-us/who-we-are/history), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/amyfeldman/2021/03/15/marie-langer-took-charge-of-her-billionaire-familys-3d-printing-firm-months-before-covid-19-struck-heres-how-she-navigated-the-downturn-and-her-plan-for-the-future/)
- ~1,000–1,250 employees across ~35 countries; last widely cited revenue €362M (FY2018/19) — current figures not public. No layoff/restructuring reports found for 2024–2025. [Statista](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/827295/umfrage/umsatz-von-electro-optical-systems-eos)

## Renishaw (Wotton-under-Edge, UK)

- Founded **1973** by Sir David McMurtry and John Deer; **public (LSE: RSW, FTSE 250), market cap ~$4.9B** (June 2026); 5,339 employees; FY2025 record revenue £713M. Financially the most solid vendor on this list — but metals AM is a small division, AM system demand was "weaker" in FY2025, and margins are being propped by a ~£20M payroll-reduction program. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renishaw_plc), [metrology.news](https://metrology.news/renishaw-delivers-record-fy2025-results-with-strong-manufacturing-growth/)
- 2021: founders (~53% stake) put the company up for sale, then took it off the market after no acceptable offers. **AM 250: discontinued and end-of-support at Renishaw**; third parties (e.g., Thinklaser) support the installed base. [TCT](https://www.tctmagazine.com/additive-manufacturing-3d-printing-news/renishaw-board-launches-formal-sales-process/), [Thinklaser](https://thinklaser.com/news/renishaw-am-250-optical-support/)

## Nikon SLM Solutions (Lübeck, Germany)

- SLM technology lineage traces to 1990s selective-laser-melting patents (Fraunhofer ILT / F&S); SLM Solutions Group AG listed on Frankfurt in 2014; **Nikon acquired it for ~€622M** (announced Sept 2022, 100% completed Jan 2023), now a wholly owned Nikon subsidiary. [Metal AM](https://www.metal-am.com/nikon-to-acquire-3d-printing-provider-slm-solutions-in-e622m-deal/), [Nikon](https://www.nikon.com/company/news/2022/slm/)
- 650+ employees; **2024 revenue €150M, +36% YoY** — one of the few Western metal-AM growth stories, thanks to the deep-pocketed parent. Portfolio shifting toward large-format (NXG XII 600, US production since June 2024), but the **SLM 125 is still in the active lineup** (no discontinuation found). [VoxelMatters](https://www.voxelmatters.com/nikon-slm-solutions-reports-revenues-of-150-million-in-2024-36/), [Nikon SLM](https://nikon-slm-solutions.com/stories-category2/slm125/)

## Farsoon Technologies (Changsha, China)

- Founded **October 2009** by Dr. Xu Xiaoshu (former head of laser sintering at 3D Systems); **IPO on Shanghai STAR Market April 2023** raising ~$155M; market cap ~$4.2B (July 2026); ~737 employees. [3DPrint.com IPO analysis](https://3dprint.com/300568/analyzing-farsoon-a-closer-look-at-the-recent-ipo-of-a-chinese-3d-printing-leader/), [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/equities/farsoon-tech)
- FY2024 dipped (revenue −19%) with the sector downturn, but **FY2025 revenue RMB 715M, +45% YoY** — strong recovery; building a new R&D headquarters; long-standing open-parameter philosophy. [VoxelMatters](https://www.voxelmatters.com/farsoon-reports-19-revenues-and-48-profit-in-fy-2024/), [TCT](https://www.tctmagazine.com/farsoon-technologies-breaks-ground-on-new-r-d-headquarters-for-industrial-3d-printing/)

## Concept Laser → GE Additive → Colibrium Additive

- Concept Laser founded **2000** in Lichtenfels, Germany by Frank Herzog ("LaserCUSING"); **GE acquired 75% for ~$599M in 2016** (after GE's bid for SLM Solutions was blocked by Elliott Management) and folded it into GE Additive alongside Arcam. [3DPrint.com](https://3dprint.com/153755/ge-acquires-concept-laser/), [GE press](https://www.geaerospace.com/news/press-releases/other-news-information/ge-reaches-agreement-acquire-75-stake-concept-laser-gmbh)
- **Rebranded Colibrium Additive April 2024** under GE Aerospace; Concept Laser and Arcam brand names retired. The M2 Series 5 remains an actively sold and supported product, but explicit support commitments for the legacy installed base (like the 2017 used M2 Series 3 quoted earlier in this thread) were not found either way — UNVERIFIED. [GE rebrand](https://www.geaerospace.com/news/press-releases/ge-additive-rebrands-colibrium-additive), [Metal AM](https://www.metal-am.com/ge-additive-rebrands-as-colibrium-additive-retires-concept-laser-and-arcam-branding/)

## Industry context 2023–2026

- The VC-era shakeout hit hard: Stratasys–Desktop Metal's $1.8B merger collapsed; Nano Dimension was court-compelled to buy Desktop Metal (~$179M, Apr 2025), which then **filed Chapter 11 in July 2025**; Nano Dimension also absorbed Markforged (~$115M); Sintratec went bankrupt (2024); ExOne/voxeljet consolidated. [Advanced Manufacturing](https://www.advancedmanufacturing.org/manufacturing-engineering/desktop-metal-files-for-bankruptcy/article_2362a415-7171-457b-a0f3-4917d7353bbb.html), [3DPrint.com 2025 recap](https://3dprint.com/322764/who-merged-who-was-acquired-and-who-disappeared-in-3d-printing-in-2025/)
- Counter-trend: vendors with deep-pocketed parents (Nikon SLM +36%, GE/Colibrium) and listed Chinese players (Farsoon +45% FY2025) kept growing while Western pure-plays contracted.
- Practical read for a purchase decision: corporate-parent or public-company vendors carry the least 5–10-year support risk; small independents (Xact, Aconity) have healthy track records but thin organizations; Mastrex is the only vendor on the list with essentially no corporate history behind the brand.
