# Preprint policy and licence, per target journal

Check this at **G1**, when the journal is chosen, and again at **G5**, before
the preprint goes up and the paper is submitted. Everything below was read
on **2026-10-10** from the sources at the bottom. APCs are list prices
excluding tax and change often. Whether BYU's library has an agreement that
covers a journal's APC is not checked here: ask the library at G1.

## At G1, for the chosen journal

- [ ] The row above still matches the journal's own guide (open it; policies move).
- [ ] Licence decided with every author: **CC BY 4.0** unless there is a
      reason not to. Fully OA journals charge the APC; for a hybrid journal,
      decide OA (APC) or subscription (copyright transfer) now, and who pays.
- [ ] If any author already published a TMS proceedings paper on this work,
      the journal paper must be a substantial extension that cites it, and
      may need TMS's permission (the TMS2027 proceedings form transfers
      copyright to TMS). ProgramMaster shows no proceedings planned for any of
      the lab's 16 abstracts.
- [ ] ASME only: the generative-AI rule (`boilerplate/generative-ai.md`).
- [ ] HardwareX only: the designs' licence is an open-hardware licence
      (CERN-OHL, TAPR OHL, or for code GPL; the guide also names CC BY-SA), and
      the design files are going to Zenodo, OSF or Mendeley Data, not GitHub
      alone.

## At G5, posting the preprint

- [ ] Every author agreed to the server and the preprint licence (sign-off
      question 9).
- [ ] The server suits the field: arXiv (cond-mat.mtrl-sci, cs, physics),
      ChemRxiv (chemistry, Digital Discovery's audience), engrXiv, or SSRN
      through Elsevier's submission system.
- [ ] Preprint licence: CC BY 4.0 by default. Springer accepts any licence but
      asks for it at submission; Elsevier's own route for accepted manuscripts
      is CC BY-NC-ND.
- [ ] The preprint is the submitted version, not a later one. After
      acceptance:
  - Elsevier: arXiv preprints may be updated to the accepted manuscript;
    elsewhere it is embargoed.
  - Springer subscription journals: never post the accepted manuscript under a
    Creative Commons licence.
  - RSC: link the preprint to the version of record; on the subscription
    route the accepted manuscript waits 12 months.
  - ASME: the accepted version may not go on a preprint server.
- [ ] Link the preprint to the published DOI once there is one (all four ask
      or encourage it).
