# External co-author sign-off

Every author outside the lab (another BYU group, the Acceleration
Consortium, a company, a facility) signs off twice: on the draft at **G3
(draft to co-authors, Jan 29)** and on the exact version submitted at **G5
(Feb 26)**. Journals ask the submitting author to confirm that every author
approved the submitted version, and that confirmation has to be true.

Silence is not consent. An author who has not answered by the deadline is
asked again, by name, and the paper waits.

## What each author confirms

Send the PDF built from a named commit (`make pdf J=<journal>`; the short
hash goes in the message) with the questions below, and ask for an answer in
writing: an email reply, or a comment on the tracker issue.

1. I have read this version (commit `_______`) in full.
2. I agree to be an author, and to the author order.
3. My name, affiliation(s), ORCID and email are correct as printed.
4. My CRediT roles in `manuscript/declarations/credit.csv` are right (each
   author checks only their own row).
5. My funding is acknowledged as my funder requires (award number, wording).
6. My conflict-of-interest declaration is accurate: none, or what it is.
7. Data, code, designs or images that came from me or my institution may be
   published under the stated licence, and need no further clearance (a
   material transfer agreement, an export-control review, a tech-transfer
   or IP review, a confidentiality agreement).
8. The generative-AI disclosure covers any AI tool I used on my part of the
   work.
9. I agree to the preprint being posted on [server] under [licence], and to
   submission to [journal].

## Record

| Author | Institution | Version approved (commit) | G3 date | G5 date | How (email date or comment link) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

Keep the emails. Paste the comment links here, or the email dates if the
approval came by email; never paste the email itself, as it may contain
addresses or other people's text.

## Timing

- Give each round 10 working days, so send the G3 draft no later than Jan 29
  and the G5 version by Feb 12 for a Feb 26 submission.
- Any change after G5 approval that touches results, author list, funding or
  declarations goes back to every author. Typo fixes do not.
