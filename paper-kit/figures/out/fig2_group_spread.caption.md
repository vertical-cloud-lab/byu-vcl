**fig2_group_spread.** **[One-sentence finding.]** Absolute error (a) and time (b) of the salt doses that ended within tolerance and inside the time limit, by how their settings were chosen. Open circles are single doses; each group's settings differ from dose to dose, so the spread is across settings, not repeatability. n = 4 (hand-tuned), 12 (screening), 3 (recentring), 12 (optimizer). Error bars: mean ± one standard deviation of the doses in the group.

Data: `data/salt_doses.csv`
