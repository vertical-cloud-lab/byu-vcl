# BYU Mechanical Engineering Christmas Service Project: 3D Printed Toys for Foster Children

its text export on 2026-11-26. The wording is theirs. Only the headings and bullets were
converted to Markdown, and the contact person's e-mail address and phone number were left out of

**November 23rd - December 18th**

The Mechanical Engineering Department invites students to use their creativity and engineering
skills to provide 3D printed toys for children in foster care this Christmas season.

## Students Can:

- Design your own toy
- Use premade 3D Printer Files
- Use your own 3D printer & materials
- Use the Prototyping Lab Printers - Some free filament will be available for this service project
- Target cost: $2-4 per item

## Recommended Limits

### Cost Limit

- $4 maximum filament cost
- Maximum filament usage: 250 grams

### Print Time Limit

- Maximum print time: 8 hours

### Materials Allowed

- PLA
- PLA+

## Suggested Approved Item Types

### Younger Children

- Flexi dragons
- Flexi dinosaurs
- Articulated sharks
- Articulated snakes
- Fidget cubes
- Puzzle toys
- Animal figures

### Older Children

- Gear puzzles
- Fidget spinners
- Infinity cubes
- Desk toys
- Mechanical puzzles
- Phone stands

### Comfort Items

- Worry stones
- Sensory fidgets
- Marble mazes
- Texture toys

## Items to Avoid

For child safety and foster-care appropriateness:

- ❌ Weapons of any kind
- ❌ Political or controversial items
- ❌ Sharp-edged models
- ❌ Choking hazards
- ❌ Models containing magnets
- ❌ Multi-part assemblies with loose small pieces

## Important Dates

- **November 23rd:** Toy Drop off and Display in the ME Office Begins
- **December 10th:** 1st Toy Delivery to Orem Foster Care office. These toys will then be
  delivered to the Richfield office.
- **December 18th:** Final Toy Delivery to Orem Foster Care Office.

## Instructions for Toy Drop off

- Bring your 3D printed toy to the ME Office (350 EB)
- Attach a gift tag to your toy with ribbon (Made with love by BYU Mechanical Engineering Students)
- Leave your name and a note on the back of the tag
- Place your toy on our toy shelf and receive a Christmas sticker to put on our toy tracking
  Christmas Tree poster

Come by often to see how many toys have been created and admire how creative our students,
Faculty and staff are.
