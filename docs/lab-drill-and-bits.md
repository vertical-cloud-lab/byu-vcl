# Lab drill and bit kits

Purchase decision for a general-purpose lab drill and bits, from
[issue #309](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/309). Prices are the last
ones found on 2026-10-28, not live ones; check them at checkout.

## Drill: DeWalt DCD793D1 (~$99 at Lowe's)

Agreed on #309. Brushless, 1/2" chuck, 20V MAX battery and charger included, 4.7★ from
791 reviews, in stock with the sale running to Jan 20. The 20V MAX battery line matters if
the lab adds an impact driver later.

Passed over:

- **Craftsman (~$88):** sale ending, but backordered to the end of November.
- **Harbor Freight Bauer (~$113):** costs more, store-brand battery line.
- **Harbor Freight Warrior 12V (~$20):** 3/8" chuck; fine only as a backup.
- **Walmart Snap Fresh kit (~$60):** 5 ratings, its own battery line, and the bits that
  come in kits like it are usually soft and go dull fast.

Not a hand-drill job: the ~3 in. deep hole in the 20 mm aluminum rods for rePowder
([Sept 17 meeting](meetings/2026-09-17-repowder-install/transcript.md)) needs a drill press
or the machine shop.

## Bit kits, ranked

Most of the lab's hardware is M3–M6 socket-head screws
([findus BOM](findus-p200-bom-build.md), [capper BOM](../modules/electromagnetic-capper/BOM.md)),
so the kit needs **metric hex bits (2.5, 3, 4 and 5 mm)**, which most US drill/drive kits
leave out.

1. **[DeWalt DW1361 21-pc titanium pilot-point drill bits](https://www.lowes.com/pd/DEWALT-21-Piece-Titanium-Twist-Drill-Bit-Set/1000502951)
   + [Milwaukee SHOCKWAVE 48-32-5155 120-pc drill & drive set](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Milwaukee-SHOCKWAVE-Impact-Duty-Alloy-Steel-Screw-Driver-Bit-Set-120-Piece-48-32-5155/325632525)**
   (~$27–38 + ~$30–60). Drill bits cover 1/16"–1/2", the drill's full chuck range; the
   Milwaukee set has the 2.5/3/4/5 mm hex bits plus Torx T10–T50, SAE hex, Phillips,
   square and slotted.
2. **[Bosch MS4091 91-pc drilling & driving set](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Bosch-Drilling-and-Driving-Set-91-Piece-MS4091/301419459)**
   (list ~$105, often $25–60 on sale). Most variety in one case: titanium, brad-point and
   masonry bits, countersink, nut drivers. Hex bits only H3, H5, H6, so M3 screws need a
   2.5 mm bit or Allen key.
3. **[Milwaukee 48-32-5155 on its own](https://www.homedepot.com/p/Milwaukee-SHOCKWAVE-Impact-Duty-Alloy-Steel-Screw-Driver-Bit-Set-120-Piece-48-32-5155/325632525)**
   (~$30–60). Cheapest way to cover every screw in the lab; only 6 drill bits (1/16"–1/4").
4. **[DeWalt TOUGHGRIP 120-pc set (DWAF120SETTG)](https://www.lowes.com/pd/DEWALT-TG-120-Pc-Drill-Drive-Bit-Set/5015604347)**
   (~$30). Same store and brand as the drill, but built for construction screws; the
   110-pc version has no hex bits.
