@vertical-cloud-lab/bambu-print-approvers: sorry for the flood of "requested your review to deploy to an environment" emails. GitHub sends one to every member of the team for each `claude-print` run, and it has no setting to turn them off.

If you'd rather not get them, set up an email filter. The steps are under [Muting the approval emails](#muting-the-approval-emails) in the issue above. For Gmail, it's a filter on `from:notifications@github.com "requested your review to deploy to an environment"` set to *Skip the Inbox*.

You'll stay on the team and can still approve runs.
