**Revised manuscript DD-ART-06-2026-000353, "Bayesian Optimization Hackathon for Chemistry and Materials"**

We submit a revised version of our manuscript for consideration in *Digital Discovery*. The
four referees valued the open, reusable outputs of the AC-BO Hackathon 2024, and they asked
above all for more synthesis: what the 45 projects reveal about Bayesian optimization in
chemistry and materials, what running the event taught us, and where the field could go
next. The revision adds that synthesis in four new sections and addresses each point of
the data review. Our point-by-point response is attached. The section, paragraph and page
numbers it gives refer to the revised manuscript, not to the marked-up version.

The main changes are:

- **Cross-Project Synthesis (Section IV):** a classification of all 45 outputs by primary
  deliverable (Table IV, with per-project assignments in ESI Table S1), licensing (Table V),
  documentation, dependencies and maintenance of every project's repository (ESI Table S2),
  and the data source behind each project.
- **Lessons Learned (Section V):** what worked and what did not in organizing the event,
  and the strengths and limitations of Bayesian optimization that the projects exposed.
- **Post-hackathon Outcomes (Section VI):** a review of public records showing which
  projects continued and what the co-authors went on to do.
- **Future Opportunities (Section VII).**
- **All 45 projects are now summarized**, including the first- and second-place projects.
  The summarization workflow now states its model, settings and generation date, and its
  raw and edited outputs are archived.
- **Figures and presentation:** participant display names are pixelated in Figures 4 and
  5, the Figure 2 labels are outlined for legibility, the project table no longer ends on
  an empty page, the PDF text now extracts correctly, and ESI Table S3 prints the full URL
  behind every link in Table III.
- **References** were checked against Crossref: incorrect DOIs and author lists were
  corrected, missing DOIs added, and preprints that have since been published are cited in
  their published form.
