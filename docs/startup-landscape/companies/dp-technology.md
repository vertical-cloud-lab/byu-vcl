# DP Technology (深势科技)

Beijing, China · founded 2018 · AI-for-materials (computational) · active · [dp.tech](https://www.dp.tech/en)

AI-for-Science platform company: Deep Potential and Uni-Mol models, the Bohrium research cloud, the Hermite drug-design and Piloteye battery-design platforms, and the Uni-Lab lab operating system.

## Hiring vs. funding

The company says each large round is mainly for hiring. The 2023 (CNY 700M) and 2025 (CNY 800M) announcements both name talent recruitment as the main use of funds. The R&D team grew from "several dozen" (2022, after the Series B) to "over a hundred" (2023, at the B+). LinkedIn put the company at 201-500 by early 2025, before the Series C. No dated posting data exists to test the timing more finely.

## Coverage and caveats

- No IPO filing was found, so there is no prospectus headcount by function. A third-party database mentions a possible Hong Kong IPO, but nothing primary does.
- OpenAlex has no institution record for DP Technology, so the publication proxy was skipped.
- The LinkedIn slug `dp-technology` belongs to an unrelated US CAM-software firm. The Beijing company is `dptechnology`.
- A campus-recruitment ad (403 on fetch) reportedly claims PhDs and postdocs are over 35% of staff. It is unverified.
- Offices are in Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen and Yibin. The Yibin centre has 30+ technical staff per [Sichuan press](https://cbgc.scol.com.cn/news/7780993). There is also a US entity, Atombeat Inc. (Jersey City).
