## Hiring vs. funding

Hiring opened on the same day as the $300M seed announcement (2025-09-30), with 12 postings live at launch. The LinkedIn member count then rose from 28 (Oct 2025) to 46 (Mar 2026), 56 (May 2026) and 88 (Aug 2026), about 3x in 11 months. Captured postings dipped to 2–4 per quarter over the 2025-26 winter. They surged to ~17 in 2026Q2 and ~16 in 2026Q3, the same period as the reported ~$7B fundraise (Bloomberg, Mar 2026) and the May 2026 SPV filing. The surge was mostly a lab build-out rather than ML hiring: technicians, equipment, EHS, process-safety and facility roles in Menlo Park. ML research is now 5 of 33 openings.

## Coverage and caveats

- LinkedIn counts are members who list the company, so they can include interns and contractors. The 11-50 band is stale.
- Wayback captures of the Ashby board are uneven, and posting quarters follow crawl dates. The launch-day board is recoverable: the archived page is gzip-encoded and embeds every posting as JSON. Its 14 roles, and the full descriptions of 30 postings that have since closed, are on the [job-postings page](../job-postings/periodic-labs.md).
- The 2026 round is press-reported only, so its amount is left blank.
- Not found: a Form D for the company, an OpenAlex institution, registry headcount, or a team page.
