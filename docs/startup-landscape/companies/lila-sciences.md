## Hiring vs. funding

Lila went from a 51-200 LinkedIn band at its March 2025 seed unveiling to 326 LinkedIn members by March 2026 and 550 by October 2026. Leadership on the team page doubled (15 to 33). Captured postings were ~22 in 2025Q4, right after the $235M Series A and $115M NVIDIA extension, then 7 in 2026Q1. They jumped to ≥100 in 2026Q2 and ≥58 in 2026Q3, around the reported ~$2B Series B talks (June 2026). The 2026 mix is heavy on software, GTM/enterprise and shift-based research-operations roles, consistent with scaling AI Science Factories toward customers.
