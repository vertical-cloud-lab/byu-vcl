## Hiring vs. funding

Thin data. The INSEE band put salaried staff at 6-9 in 2024, after the EUR 3.7M pre-seed, and LinkedIn showed 12 people in February 2025. The October 2025 seed (USD 10M) was explicitly for expanding the labs and growing the team, but there is no post-seed headcount datapoint to test that against. The pre-seed plan was an automated lab by end-2024; the seed plan is gram-to-industrial scale-up within two years.
