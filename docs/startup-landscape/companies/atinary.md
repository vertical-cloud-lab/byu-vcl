## Turnover

No leadership departures, layoffs or pivots found. Both founders are still CEO and CTO. The business has broadened from SDLabs software to operating its own autonomous lab in Boston (opened early 2026), which shows in the Boston lab-technician opening.

## Hiring vs. funding

Atinary has stayed lean. LinkedIn showed 17 people nine months after the USD 5M AgFunder seed and 22 two years after it, about 2-3 net hires a year. The current burst of four postings (August to October 2026) coincides with Bruker's minority investment (announced 14 August 2026) and the ramp-up of the Boston lab, which is consistent with the strategic money funding a modest expansion rather than a step change.
