## Hiring vs. funding

The USD 52M Series A (2025-12) came three months after the USD 11M seed. Every currently open role was posted 4-10 months later, at about 6-7 a quarter and still rising. The mix is weighted toward wet-lab scientists and research associates (6 of 15) plus robotics hardware and test (3). That matches the stated use of funds, "growing engineering and scientific teams" and scaling biopharma deployments, and suggests a build-out of in-house lab capacity (a "Foundry") rather than pure ML hiring.
