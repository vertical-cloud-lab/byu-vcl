# Matlantis Corporation (formerly Preferred Computational Chemistry)

Tokyo, Japan · founded 2021 · AI-for-materials (computational) · active · [matlantis.com](https://matlantis.com/en/)

A joint venture of Preferred Networks (PFN) and ENEOS that sells Matlantis, a universal atomistic simulator built on the PFP neural-network potential, as cloud SaaS. Mitsubishi Corporation joined as a shareholder in 2024. The company was renamed from PFCC on 2025-07-01.

## Hiring vs. funding

There are no discrete funding events to correlate with. The company is funded as a corporate JV, and paid-in capital has stayed at JPY 310M. Its growth follows commercial milestones instead: paid service and US servers in 2023, Mitsubishi's distribution alliance in 2024, a Cambridge, MA office in 2024-09 and the first US employee in 2025. At 46 staff in mid-2025, its current openings are in sales and product, not research. Model R&D largely stays at parent PFN.
