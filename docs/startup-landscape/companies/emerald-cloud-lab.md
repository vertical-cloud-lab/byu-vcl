## Turnover

- **2023-04:** a California WARN notice for 30 employees at South San Francisco (notice 2023-04-14, effective 2023-04-30, listed as a closure). It came with the relocation to Austin, and staff were invited to relocate and reapply ([WARN Tracker](https://www.warntracker.com/layoff/emerald-cloud-lab-2023-04-30), [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/?p=221214)).
- **Laboratory Operations shrank across the move.** The team-page group went 40 → 33 → 23 → 8 between 2020-09 and 2023-07, and it was 6 in 2026-10. Scientific Development and Design & Engineering held or grew.
- **Board:** Luke Nosek appears on the 2010 and 2012 Form Ds but not the 2014 one.
- No other named leadership departures were found. The founders have been co-CEOs throughout.

## Hiring vs. funding

The team page has stayed at about 90-110 people since 2020, with no clear step after any round. The largest rise, 89 (2021-12) to 108 (2022-12), came in the year of the reported June 2022 Series C and the CMU cloud-lab build. It was almost all Scientific Development, which went from 25 to 43. The 2023 Austin relocation then swapped on-site lab operators for other roles: Lab Ops fell from 23 to 8 under a 30-person WARN notice while the total held at 107, and a Marketing & Sales group appeared. The reported April 2024 Series B extension was followed by a dip to 94, not growth.
