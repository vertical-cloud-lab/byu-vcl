## Turnover

No departures of founders or executives found. The three founders are still the only UK directors. The only registry resignations are incorporation-era service providers: a nominee director/secretary (2022-11-14) and the corporate secretary The Briars Group (2024-01-31) ([CH officers](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/14332843/officers)). No layoffs reported. The 2026 rebrand also shifted the product focus from materials discovery to AI data-centre hardware: cooling fluids and a modular compute platform.

## Hiring vs. funding

The team went from 13 at the February 2024 Series A to about 50 at the May 2026 Series B, roughly fourfold in 27 months. The UK entity stayed flat at 8 average employees in 2024 and 2025, so most of that growth was in the US: the Princeton NJ wet lab (chemistry researcher ads in mid-2025) and then San Francisco. Captured postings were steady at roughly 2-9 per quarter through 2025 and the first half of 2026, peaking in 2025Q4 (9). That was a year after the NVentures investment, not right after it. Six of the seven current openings were published in August-September 2026, three months after the Series B. They are forward-deployed and backend engineering plus direct-to-chip hardware roles rather than chemists, which matches the pivot to data-centre products.
