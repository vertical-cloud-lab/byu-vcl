## Turnover

- Co-founder and CSO Bryce Meredig left around 2022. He was quoted as CSO in April 2022, is absent from the Form D related persons from November 2022, and a [current bio](https://www.deep-tech-week.com/speakers/7d39d607-2480-41d7-b555-cee368ab775a) names Travertine Labs.
- Five of the seven functional leads on the [2019 leadership list](https://web.archive.org/web/20190721232539/https://citrine.io/about/), plus CTO Julia Ling, are gone from the [2023 list](https://web.archive.org/web/20231203144008/https://citrine.io/about/). The five are Engineering, Scientific Software, Operations, Marketing and Product.
- COO Justine Trubey and CCO Scott Carlyle (both on the 2023 list) are absent from the [live page](https://citrine.io/about/). The COO is now Kyle Killebrew.
- LinkedIn-listed employees fell from 84 (Feb 2024) to 69 (Dec 2024) and have held at 68-71 since.

## Hiring vs. funding

Leadership hires cluster just after raises. Two VPs of Sales arrived four months after the June 2017 Series A announcement. A CTO and a VP Global Sales came a month after the October 2019 $20M Series B, and a COO four months later. The team grew 19% in Q4 2020, the quarter of the $12.35M Series B extension. The CFO and CCO were hired the same month the $16M Series C was announced (January 2023). After the Series C, raises shrank to $5.0M (2023) and $2.6M (2025), and LinkedIn-listed staff fell by about 18% during 2024. Captured postings ran at about one per quarter in 2025 and rose to two or three per quarter in mid-2026, without a matching announced round.
