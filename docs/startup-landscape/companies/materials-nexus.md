## Hiring vs. funding

Average headcount roughly doubled each year after the July 2023 seed: 4 in 2022, 9 in 2023, 17 in 2024 and 27 in 2025. The seed release itself said the team had "doubled in the last six months", so hiring ran ahead of the round's close. The second step (17 to 27) follows the January 2025 allotments, which raised share capital by 39%, an unannounced round. By October 2026 the team grid had settled at 25 with no open roles, a pause after two years of near-doubling.
