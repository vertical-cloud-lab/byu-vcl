## Turnover

- Founder-CEO Jill Becker was quoted as CEO in March 2021 but is absent from the [2023 Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1749021/000174902123000001/primary_doc.xml). By then the executive officers were Ashish Kulkarni, Semion Saikin, Dennis Sheberla and Aspuru-Guzik. A [2026 release](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/930102663/arradiance-appoints-dr-jill-becker-to-board-of-directors) describes Kebotix as her past venture.
- Directors Dukach and Dharmadhikari left the board between the 2020 and 2023 filings. Aspuru-Guzik is absent from the [2024 Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1749021/000174902124000001/primary_doc.xml).
