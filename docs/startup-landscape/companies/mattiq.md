## Turnover

- **CEO change.** Jeff Erhardt was CEO on the 2022 and 2024 Form Ds and in the [2025-01-30 Heraeus release](https://www.heraeus-precious-metals.com/en/company/press-and-news/mattiq-and-heraeus-partner-to-advance-electrocatalyst-innovation/). By 2026-10 the site lists Ben Schlatka as CEO. The exact date was not found.
- **Seed-era board turned over.** Louis Simpson and Alex Andrianapoulis were directors on the 2020 Form D and are absent from the 2022 one ([Form D](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1843720/000184372022000002/primary_doc.xml)).

## Hiring vs. funding

There are not enough headcount or posting data to relate hiring to rounds. Leadership changes do track the filings: a professional CEO (Erhardt) and a VC director (Roberts, Material Impact) arrived with the 2022 raise and the rename. A commercial chief followed in 2024-10, two months before the small December 2024 insider round, which added a CS Venture director. The site has since repositioned from "clean chemistry" catalysts to a materials ground-truth data engine for AI, under a new CEO.
