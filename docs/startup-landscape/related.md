# Related issues and pull requests

These are the threads in this repo (byu-vcl) and in [digital-alloy-lab](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab) (DAL) that this survey builds on, overlaps with, or could feed. On 2026-11-28 I read every issue and pull request in both repos, open and closed: titles, bodies and comments. A thread is listed here only if it shares companies, funding or staffing questions with the survey, or the methods it used.

## Where the survey comes from

- **[byu-vcl#155](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/155)** (issue, open): *Explore precedent for enterprise / academic interactions.* This PR answers the request posted there on 2026-10-09.
- **[byu-vcl#156](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/156)** (PR, closed without merging): the first answer to #155. Its `enterprise-academic-precedents.md` covers industry consortia, shared facilities such as the Carnegie Mellon–Emerald Cloud Lab partnership, and VC and spinout models. This survey follows on at the level of individual companies.

## Same companies, funding or staffing

- **[byu-vcl#380](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/380)** (issue) and **[byu-vcl#386](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/386)** (PR): *The value of open science in the age of AI.*
  - The first round compares organizations by how open they are, over time. It includes Periodic Labs ($300M at founding, betting on closed, self-generated lab data) and finds that the 2025–26 AI-for-science startups raised about $9B, nearly all of it for closed work.
  - #386 adds an investor's-eye memo and lists 1,955 funders of open-source-minded ventures, with every round in `deals.csv`.
  - Both datasets record funding rounds for some of the same startups, so they can be checked against each other.
- **[byu-vcl#343](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/343)** (issue) and **[byu-vcl#344](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/344)** (PR): *Facility locations.*
  - Its first comparable is Radical AI's new lab: 45,000 ft², not the reported 40,000, in Building 20 at the Brooklyn Navy Yard, moved into around September 2026. That matches [Radical AI's profile](companies/radical-ai.md) here, which has the $4M Navy Yard project, ~34 staff and 115 committed jobs.
  - Its building-needs table puts staffing at one person on the weekday day shift, rising to 5–6 FTE at ten alloys a day ([comment](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/344#issuecomment-6962374367)). The posted technician and operator pay in [`job-postings/`](job-postings/README.md) can put a price on those positions.
- **[byu-vcl#106](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/106)** (issue) and **[byu-vcl#107](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/107)** (PR): *A recharge model for the VCL.*
  - Its comments conclude that staff effort, not depreciation, sets a realistic recharge rate: about $601 a run staffed at 1.0 FTE and $896 at 2.0 FTE, against a draft rate of $306.
  - It also found that Emerald Cloud Lab-style prepaid credits run into BYU's rule against billing in advance.
  - ECL's staffing history here (laboratory operations fell 40 → 6, 2020–26) and its posted pay are direct inputs: a laboratory operator at $20–26 an hour, a shift manager at $70–90K. See [ECL's profile](companies/emerald-cloud-lab.md) and [its postings](job-postings/emerald-cloud-lab.md).
- **[byu-vcl#407](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/issues/407)** (issue) and **[byu-vcl#410](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/410)** (PR): *Is the vertical lab worth it?* Its case studies include Emerald Cloud Lab and Strateos. ECL's profile here shows how many people a cloud lab takes to run, and in which roles.
- **The digital twin:**
  - **[byu-vcl#393](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/393)**: rounds 1–2.
  - **[byu-vcl#405](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/405)**: round 5, *two years from the day the money arrives*. It puts all the money and the fixed staff in place on day 0, and readies the facility between day 60 and day 100 (a stand-in). The companies surveyed here took two to three quarters after a round to grow their teams ([finding 2](README.md#findings)). A hiring ramp would therefore make a fair sensitivity.
  - **[byu-vcl#427](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/427)**: round 6, where money is tracked rather than limited. The hiring-ramp sensitivity would fit here.
  - **[byu-vcl#406](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/byu-vcl/pull/406)**: the floor-space video, which replaces #404. It uses ECL's published facility sizes as its reference: about 15,000 ft² in South San Francisco and 105,000 ft² in Austin.
- **[DAL#15](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/15)** (issue, closed) and **[DAL#16](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/16)** (PR, merged before DAL moved repos; its number is a placeholder now): *Company landscape for digital alloy discovery*, now [`company-landscape.md`](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/blob/8477dee/company-landscape.md).
  - It overlaps this survey only at Citrine Informatics, and is out of date there. It dates Citrine's Series C to 2025 and lists Bryce Meredig as CEO.
  - According to the Form Ds and press releases in [Citrine's profile](companies/citrine-informatics.md):
    - The $16M Series C was announced in January 2023. The later raises, $5.0M in 2023 and $2.6M in 2025, were unannounced.
    - Greg Mulholland is CEO.
    - Meredig, co-founder and Chief Science Officer, left around 2022.
  - The rest of that list (powder makers, AM machine vendors, ICME software) falls outside this survey.
- **[DAL#133](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/133)** (issue): *Potential acquisition of a laser powder bed fusion printer.* Its comments size up LPBF vendors the way this survey sizes up startups, reading headcount and funding as signs of stability. Xact Metal has ~15 people and ~$250K of disclosed funding. One Click Metal has ~20 people, 2oneLab 2–10, and Aconity3D 51–200.
- **[DAL#83](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/issues/83)** (issue) and **[DAL#84](https://github.com/vertical-cloud-lab/digital-alloy-lab/pull/84)** (PR): grants funded by NSF, DOE, DoD and NASA across six topics. This is the public-money counterpart to the venture rounds tracked here.
