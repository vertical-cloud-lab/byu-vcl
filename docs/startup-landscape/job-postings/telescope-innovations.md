### Product Manager (2026-11-16)

**Role Summary**
The Product Manager owns the commercial success of the DirectInject-LC and adjacent product lines. The role is market-facing and business-focused: understanding customers and markets, defining what to build and why, leading new products through Telescope's New Product Development and Introduction (NPDI) process to a successful launch, and driving sales, growth and margin. The Product Manager works alongside the Product Specialist, who provides technical product expertise, testing, documentation and technical customer support.

Reporting structure and time-commitment

Reports to SVP Sales and Marketing
Full time
Indefinite term
In-person role at [phone removed] East Mall

Job duties

Market Research and Customer Insight

Conduct ongoing market research and competitive analysis across pharmaceutical and fine and specialty chemical segments to identify customer needs, market trends and growth opportunities
Define and maintain market segmentation by industry, application and account
Gather voice-of-customer input from customers, distribution partners and sales teams, and translate it into clear market and product requirements
Size target markets and build business cases to support product line growth and new product investment decisions

Product Strategy and Roadmap

Own the product line strategy and multi-year roadmap to grow the product line vertically and horizontally across target segments
Define product positioning and value propositions for each target segment
Prioritize new products, upgrades and features based on market opportunity, customer value and business return

New Product Development and Introduction (NPDI)

Lead new product opportunities from ideation through launch using Telescope's NPDI program, including stage-gate reviews and the standard product readiness checklist
Define market and product requirements, target pricing and target cost, and own the business case for each new product
Partner with R&D, Manufacturing and the Product Specialist, who leads technical validation, testing and transfer to manufacturing, to keep projects on time and aligned with market needs
Plan and lead product launches, including go-to-market strategy, launch timing, and readiness of sales, service and distribution partners
Track post-launch commercial performance against the business case and recommend corrective actions

Marketing and Go-to-Market

Work with Telescope Marketing Communications to develop and release marketing programs that build product awareness, generate leads and support opportunity development
Work with distribution partner marketing teams to develop and execute high-value joint marketing activities that support business development
Own product messaging, sales tools and commercial collateral

Product Line Business Management

Accountable for product line sales, growth and margin targets; agree unit and revenue targets with senior management
