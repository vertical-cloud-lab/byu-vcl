Posting for Sterling from the 9/17 call with Bartosz ([corrected transcript]({{TRANSCRIPT}})).

Asked about the cheaper acrylic boxes with an antechamber. His take: most of AMAZEMET's customers don't use a glovebox at all; a clean handling area is enough. What matters is storage: proper sealed containers (not a plastic tub) with silica gel packets, otherwise the powder clumps and oxidizes over time. They only use a glovebox when packing samples to ship, then vacuum-bag; well-passivated powder tolerates a bit of air.

Still worth pricing an enclosure-style box up to ~$4–5k (#78, #217).
