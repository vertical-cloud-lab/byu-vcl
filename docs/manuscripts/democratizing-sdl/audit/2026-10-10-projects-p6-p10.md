## Headline findings

1. **P7 does have public artefacts, but its blocking status is only narrowed, not cleared.** The RDE adapter STLs are at `ethraj2001/jubilee` @ `d5c5969` (2024-08-27) and were proposed upstream as machineagency/jubilee#204, which is still open and unmerged. A BluRev RDE tool class and config are at `cyrilcaoyang/jubilee-sdl2` @ `bc548db` (2025-02-13; archived fork, MIT). None of it covers potentiostat/CV control, a BOM, documentation or a DOI. A consolidated deposit approved by the authors would clear the objection.
2. **No P7 publication exists for 2024–2026** in Crossref or OpenAlex. The only hit is this manuscript's own preprint.
3. **P8 has a citable code and CAD home.** It is `AccelerationConsortium/ac-dev-lab/src/ac_training_lab/picow/digital-pipette` (MIT): MQTT firmware, time-sync scripts and Hattrick's two STLs.
