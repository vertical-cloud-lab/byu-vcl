# Per-author checklist: contributions, the files behind them, and what each person confirms

**Manuscript:** "Replication, not fabrication: documentation is the rate-limiting step for democratized self-driving labs" (revision of *Digital Discovery* DD-PER-12-2024-000410). **Corresponding author:** Sterling G. Baird.
**State on 27 November 2026:** no co-author has signed off yet. Nothing has been received on the PR thread or in the repository since the v3 package went up on 10 October 2026.

Each author has a section below with four parts:

1. **Contribution:** the author's CRediT role(s), word for word from the manuscript.
2. **Where it appears:** the parts of the manuscript that rest on that work.
3. **Files that back it:** the files in this folder, and the public records outside it, that show the contribution.
4. **Confirm:** the items the author must answer. An ID such as `TV-1` also appears as an HTML comment next to the sentence it governs in [`../manuscript-v3.md`](../manuscript-v3.md), and [`../sign-off-checklist.md`](../sign-off-checklist.md) gives the full wording. In the Word review copy (`review-copies/manuscript-v3-for-review.docx`) the IDs appear inline as **[[TV-1]]**, so a search for your ID finds the sentences.

Project-level evidence (repositories, figures, table rows and audit notes) is collected once per project in the [project evidence packs](#project-evidence-packs) at the end, and each author section links to its pack.

**Seven items every author confirms** (shown as "ALL-1 to ALL-7" below):

| ID | Confirm |
| --- | --- |
| ALL-1 | You have read the manuscript, approve it, and agree to its submission to *Digital Discovery* with you as an author (RSC requires every author's agreement). |
| ALL-2 | Your full affiliation: department, institution, city, postcode, country. |
| ALL-3 | Your CRediT role(s), as worded in your section. |
| ALL-4 | No conflict of interest, or the one to declare (including anything new since 2024, e.g. selling kits or instruments described here). |
| ALL-5 | Your funding acknowledgement (the list is v1's and known to be incomplete). |
| ALL-6 | The title. |
| ALL-7 | Your ORCID iD (required for the submitting author, encouraged for all). |
