# The furnace, refined against Nabertherm's NAT video (#303, PR #318)

The model was a single stainless box with a black controller strip, a green switch and a black lever latch. Nabertherm's
own video of the NAT series shows the NAT 15/65 itself, from the front, with its door being opened:
[Discover the NAT Series: High Uniformity, Compact Design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WE6O8EccLyc).
- [0:16](https://youtu.be/WE6O8EccLyc?t=16): the whole furnace, from the front, as the door opens.
- [0:20](https://youtu.be/WE6O8EccLyc?t=20): a close, nearly square-on view of the open furnace.

## What it showed, and what changed

- **Two units, not one box.** A stainless chamber unit sits on a separate controller unit, about 149 mm tall. The
  controller has a wide touchscreen (about 228 × 75 mm) in the middle of its front, not a strip with a screen at the
  right. The power switch is a small black rocker at the right, under a yellow label.
- **A vent strip** of five slots runs along the top front, above the door.
- **The door.** It is a stainless panel with a thick white fibre plug behind it.
  - The plug fills the chamber's mouth, then steps into the chamber.
  - It hangs on two hinge brackets at the right. That hinge side was already right.
  - Its handle is a vertical bar near the left edge, not a black lever.
  - A small knob on the left side latches it.
- **The fan guard** in the back wall is small, about 72 mm across, with concentric rings and spokes. It was 130 mm.
- **The chamber's mouth** is about 329 × 170 mm. Its floor is 226 mm above the bench, and it is centred.

## The fit

The published 470 × 790 × 460 mm is the scale. The two frames come from different shots of an edited video, so each
has its own camera (`"camera"` in the view files).

| View | Picks | RMS |
|---|---|---|
| 0:16, the whole furnace | upper unit's corner, the split, foot, display corners | 1.2 px |
| 0:20, close, door open | mouth corners, top corner, split, fan centre, display top | 6.3 px |

Both views are close to square-on, so depth is weakly constrained. The fit sets heights and widths on the front;
depths come from the published 790 mm and the 15 L chamber.

## Still uncertain

- **The chamber's inside** (300 × 340 × 150 mm, for 15 L) is not measured.
- **The plug's depth** is read off the open door, about 60 mm.
