# The XRF, refined against Bruker's video (#303, PR #318)

The model was a sloped white body with a small dark lid and the touch screen on top, drawn from photos. Bruker's own
[S2 PUMA Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ogSg0j6j2Gw) shows the XY Autochanger, closed and with its hood
opening ([0:08–0:16](https://youtu.be/ogSg0j6j2Gw?t=8)).

## What it showed, and what changed

- **The hood is full width, white, and covers the top 47 % of the height** (from 322 mm up to 610 mm). It has a dark
  window in its top and BRUKER at its front right. It lifts on a rear hinge.
- **The front is vertical, not sloped.** It is white, with the touch screen (about 253 × 184 mm) set into it just
  left of centre. "S2 PUMA" runs up its right edge.
- **Navy side panels** run the height of the body, under the hood, with dark feet below.

The internals and the motion are unchanged: the tray, the cup, the tube and detector, the beam and the spectrum.

## The fit

One view, 0:09, with the published 670 × 710 × 610 mm as the scale: RMS 12.7 px.
- **The worst pick is the hood's top front-right corner** (31 px). Its far top corner sits higher in the video than
  a square box predicts, so the hood's top may be slightly bevelled.
- **Fitted:** the hood's split height and the screen's size and place.
- **Not fitted:** the window's size and place, which were set by eye.

## Still uncertain

- **This is a render from Bruker, not a photo.** Its proportions should be Bruker's CAD's, but are not checked against
  a real unit.
- **The hood's open height.** The model opens it 35°, so its front edge rises to about 1010 mm; open, it needs the
  VLM's opening.
