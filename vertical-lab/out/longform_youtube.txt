A design study of an automated alloy lab at BYU's Vertical Cloud Lab: two Kardex vertical lift modules hold the instruments on powered trays, and UR12e arms (the UR10e, renamed in 2025) on MiR250 mobile bases work at their openings and at the floor machines. The alloy route runs from machining the charge cups through atomizing, printing, testing and characterization; then safety, upkeep, the floor and the waste, and the way from one alloy a day to ten.

New in this version (round 16): the waste handled on screen, a bottle's trip to the fume hood and a person changing a vacuum's liner at the powder-waste point by the atomizer; the machining cell on the floor; the CubXL's P300 in the powder test; the clips round 15 kept from earlier renders, the set-up movie, the finale and chapters 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7 drawn again on the current lab. The rest are round 15's renders.

A 3-D animation, not footage of a working lab: makers' published sizes and photos, our own designs where nothing exists yet, every arm move checked for collisions. Real footage, photos and data are badged; the voice is synthetic (Kokoro).
