Everything on PR #318 of the BYU Vertical Cloud Lab, in one video: a Kardex Shuttle XP 500 vertical lift module holding the lab's equipment on electrified trays, a UR10e on a MiR250 at its opening, the alloy route from charge cups to a tensile bar, every instrument doing its job, and the alternatives. 3-D animation built from makers' published figures and photos; real footage is badged as such.
