# A digital twin of the manufacturing network, for synthetic benchmarks (work in progress)

Two alloy campaigns, run at the same time and kept apart: FLAIME's aluminium alloys for laser powder bed fusion
(DAL, Genesis Mission proposal) and the ONR YIP proposal's copper alloys for blown-powder DED (DAL PR 117), each on
its own search space, function network, objectives and budget, sharing the lab of PR 318 and outside services, with
lead times and a market whose prices drift and jump.
