## Drilling the arm's plate

Two Ø1/4 in holes 15 mm deep for the dowels (the plate is 18 mm, so not through), at -391 and +391 mm along the shelf and 450 mm from J1 toward the wall. Rather than measuring them out, mark them from the legs: drill the legs first, drop a 1/4 in dowel centre into each hole and press the shelf down where it goes. The two sets then line up whatever the error in marking out.
