# References and how each was checked

Every DOI below resolved on Crossref on 2026-10-13, and its title, authors, year and venue match
the citation. **Read** says what the claim in this folder rests on:

- **FT**: an open-access full text
- **abs**: the abstract
- **meta**: metadata only. The paper is cited for what its title says, or for a fact quoted by a
  source that *was* read (named in the row).

Publisher pages (Elsevier, Wiley, ACS, RSC) block the runner, so nothing paywalled was read in full.

## Standards (titles from Crossref records, DOI 10.1520/&lt;id&gt;)

- **G5**: potentiodynamic anodic polarization (reference test method).
- **G59**: potentiodynamic polarization resistance.
- **G61**: cyclic potentiodynamic polarization for localized corrosion susceptibility of **iron-,
  nickel- or cobalt-based alloys**. Hence "G61-style" here.
- **G102**: calculating corrosion rates from electrochemical measurements.
- **G1**: preparing, cleaning and evaluating corrosion test specimens.
- **B117**: salt spray (fog) apparatus.
- **G31**: laboratory immersion corrosion testing (a guide).
- **E34**: chemical analysis of aluminum and aluminum-base alloys.

G150, the critical pitting temperature test, is for stainless steels. Frankel notes that Al alloys
show no critical pitting temperature in aqueous chloride down to 0 °C, so it isn't cited here.
